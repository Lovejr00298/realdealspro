Recover surplus funds, step by step

  1. Sign in under Settings. Owner names only appear when you're signed in.
  2. Go to Find money → Surplus cases, pick a county and review the list. Larger amounts first.
  3. Click Add to my cases on the ones you want to work.
  4. Click Find owner for mailing addresses, then Get phone and email (paid lookup). Check Do Not Call flags before calling.
  5. Open the Clerk report link and confirm the money is still unclaimed, who is entitled, and the deadline.
  6. Contact the owner and set the case stage to Owner contacted.
  7. Go to Documents, fill in the fee agreement and click Send for e-signature. Text or email the link. Once the owner signs, the case moves to Agreement signed by itself.
  8. File the claim with the clerk and set the stage to Claim filed, then Paid when the money arrives.

Find and sell real estate deals

  1. Find deals → Property search: choose a state and county, add distress filters, and save the properties you like.
  2. Ranked deals: approve your best deals.
  3. Buyers & outreach: add real buyers with what they buy, then send approved deals to the buyers who match.

Foreclosures

Find money → Upcoming foreclosures lists clerk sales. Click Track case to follow one. After the sale date passes, it appears under Surplus cases for you to check.

Important: County amounts are what the clerk reports, not a guarantee. Some counties (DeKalb, Chatham) only pay owners who file themselves or use a Georgia attorney. Have an attorney review agreements and outreach wording before you use them.
